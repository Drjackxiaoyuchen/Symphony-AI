# Medication source-plan review v1

Method `symphony-medication-plan-v1` · 2026-11-22. The UI uses the existing Symphony AI copper/ivory scheme and remains a proposal awaiting the user's acceptance.

## Endpoint, estimand and scope

The estimand is the **source-listed planned ingredient quantity on a selected calendar date, conditional on the entered current source plan**. It is not observed consumption, absorbed/systemic exposure, adherence, recommended dosing, dose suitability or clinical risk. This release removes the Medicine Checker page's illustrative drug/genotype recommendations and interaction claims. Drug–drug interactions, contraindications, allergies and personal pharmacogenomics have **no evaluated coverage**. An absent flag is not evidence of safety.

Records are manually transcribed from labels/documents and are not independently verified. They contain product name, form/release description, route, label unit, units per administration, ingredient strengths per one label unit, schedule, current status, first/last planned day, original instructions, source/URL, notes and source-check instant. No model, external service, terminology database, FHIR importer, e-prescribing operation or raw genotype analysis is installed. Supplement/family ledgers are separate inputs and are not incorporated here.

## Input gates and missingness

- Strict Zod entry and `symphony-ledger-v1` / `medications` wrapper schemas, unique IDs, maximum 5,000 records; maximum 20 ingredients per product. Amounts/units per administration may be null but, when known, must be positive finite numbers. Numerical caps are computational bounds, **not clinical dose thresholds**. Unsupported amount dimensions (including percent concentrations) are rejected; they require explicit source conversion/review before entry.
- Ingredient strength is per **one** selected unit (tablet, capsule, mL, drop, puff, patch, application or other unit). For a label of 25 mg/5 mL, enter 5 mg/mL and preserve the original instruction text. No automatic concentration parser is provided. Strength and administered unit quantity remain separate. Known zero strength is rejected; an unknown quantity stays null, not zero.
- Fixed daily schedules require a positive integer administration count, with fractional unit quantities allowed. Non-daily, mixed scheduled-plus-PRN, alternating, tapering, infusion, calendar-specific or complex instructions are **unresolved**, with source text retained. No average daily frequency or nearest-number inference is made.
- As-needed (PRN) schedules retain an optional positive integer source maximum administrations/day. Missing maximum remains unknown. This is not a recommendation or a validated safe maximum.
- First/last planned dates are real ISO calendar dates; last day is inclusive and cannot precede a known first day. Active-plan records need a known first day and overlapping date for inclusion. Missing start dates, before/after-window records, held and stopped records have explicit exclusion reasons. The **current** record status applies to every review date. A past-date review is not reconstruction of historical use/status. A missing last day means no recorded end, not verified indefinite treatment.
- Each case/whitespace-normalized ingredient label can occur only once within one product; duplicate quantities from two representations of one label could otherwise be double counted. Multiple products are retained independently and can trigger a repeated-label flag.

## Deterministic calculation and assumptions

For ingredient j in an included product k:

`a_jk = source strength per label unit × entered label units per administration`.

The unit is the ingredient label (case-folded and whitespace-folded only), route and amount dimension. Only mass is normalized: 1 g = 1,000 mg; 1 mcg = 0.001 mg. IU, units, mEq and mmol remain separate. No substance-specific potency, salt/free-base, molecular-weight, bioavailability or release-form equivalence is inferred. Brands, generic names, spelling variants, salts and synonyms are not resolved through RxNorm or another ontology. Matching labels can still be incorrectly transcribed; unmatched labels can represent the same therapy.

**Known scheduled subtotal:** sum `a_jk × daily administration count` over supported fixed-daily products with known quantities. A daily product with missing strength/unit quantity or an unresolved schedule marks that aggregate as incomplete; its unknown contribution is not guessed. The displayed zero is only the known subtotal, never evidence of no exposure. Original nulls and every source contribution are preserved.

**Optional PRN ceiling:** sum `a_jk × source maximum administrations/day` for supported PRN products, kept separate from scheduled quantity. Any missing PRN amount/maximum, or unresolved regimen whose PRN status cannot be established, makes this ceiling null. PRN-only products contribute zero scheduled quantity. A zero optional ceiling for fixed-daily-only records means no entered PRN component, not zero medication use. Source maxima from different products are not independently checked for compatibility; no combined safe-use ceiling is claimed.

**Repeated-label flag:** at least two distinct included product IDs contain the same normalized ingredient label and route. This flags across amount dimensions too, while their numeric totals remain separate. It is a label inspection flag, not proof of duplicate therapy or an interaction. Form/release distinctions remain visible in contributions even when ingredient mass is arithmetically added.

No probability, confidence interval, clinical classifier or treatment recommendation is produced. Sensitivity to missing fields is exposed through null PRN ceilings, incomplete scheduled subtotals and explicit source exclusions. These fields cannot identify unrecorded medications or quantify uncertainty in transcription, actual intake or bioavailability. Calculations use JavaScript binary64; the UI displays eight significant digits while JSON retains unrounded values.

## Interface, reproducibility and privacy

The page starts empty. Manual edits require a source-check acknowledgement; save failures are shown. Add/edit, search, remove/undo and reviewed backup replacement work locally. Source records and ingredient review paginate 20 rows; full outputs retain all records/contributions. Detailed arithmetic identifies the denominator, multiplier, normalized quantity, source, form and source-check date. Inclusion/exclusion reasons are inspectable.

Storage is unencrypted browser localStorage, `symphony:medications:v1`; this is not a secure clinical record vault or reliable backup. Keep original sources and export intentionally. Reviewed JSON restoration validates before replacing data and preserves the current ledger when invalid; raw backup supports recovery from unreadable storage. CSV contains the selected date, unit, scheduled completeness, nullable PRN ceiling, record IDs and source-contribution JSON. It contains review quantities only; use full JSON for excluded source records. CSV formula-prefix escaping is retained.

Personal report v2 includes all saved source records and a daily review on the **UTC calendar date of report generation**, independent of the date selected on the Medicine Checker page. The report declares this basis and current-status limitation. An unreadable/unready medication ledger blocks full JSON download rather than silently omitting it. No illustrative values fill personal outputs.

## Primary-source review and selection

Accessed 2026-11-22. Three selected reference bundles informed this release; no source code, weights or third-party data were copied or executed.

1. **HL7 FHIR R4 v4.0.1:** [MedicationStatement](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/medicationstatement.html) and [Dosage](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/dosage-definitions.html). Its distinction between a reported statement and actual administration informed the endpoint; dosage text, timing, as-needed flag and dose quantity informed explicit fields and unresolved-schedule gates. Documentation is published under HL7's [license](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/license.html); no FHIR payload/validator integration or conformance is claimed.
2. **Medplum**, [commit 88af41c092baed5ad35dfa603df17353d3e33e48](https://github.com/medplum/medplum/tree/88af41c092baed5ad35dfa603df17353d3e33e48), specifically [useMedicationOrder.ts](https://github.com/medplum/medplum/blob/88af41c092baed5ad35dfa603df17353d3e33e48/packages/react-hooks/src/useMedicationOrder/useMedicationOrder.ts). Apache-2.0 header/license inspected. The source checks response contracts and isolates project-scoped vendor-backed search/order operations. Adopted explicit source/contract boundaries and surfaced validation errors; no Medplum/vendor backend, medication search, prescription/order operation or source code added.
3. **MedExtractor candidate screening:** [Hugging Face card](https://huggingface.co/lion-ai/medextractor-nano/tree/502b731aeafe44ae802f87134c7b969d0d7acb9f), revision `502b731aeafe44ae802f87134c7b969d0d7acb9f`. Despite the URL, this revision identifies itself as **MedExtractor Liquid**. The card describes an encoder span/null extractor and reports evaluation on synthetic clinical notes; its limitations do not establish real-world clinical validity or dose-arithmetic validity. Metadata, README training/evaluation/limitations and LICENSE were inspected. The card identifies LFM Open License v1.0 plus non-commercial training-data restrictions and asks that the release be treated as research/non-commercial. **Deferred**, with no weights/code executed, term acceptance, benchmark reproduction or integration obligation.

Additional paper screening: [Selvaraj & Konam, arXiv:1912.04961v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.04961v3), published workshop proceedings (W3PHIAI/AAAI 2020), was reviewed for grounded transcript annotations, dosage/frequency tasks, pointer-generator methods, data split and ROUGE/qualitative evaluations. Their processing removes dosage units in some evaluation settings; these scores therefore cannot validate our unit-aware quantity endpoint. It supports retaining source context and separating extraction from calculation. No corpus or benchmark was reproduced; paper accessibility is not a code/data reuse license. bioRxiv searches did not identify a sufficiently matched method to adopt for this bounded source-plan release.

## Validation and limits of evidence

`tests/medications.test.ts` adds 12 reference tests. The invented ten-record fixture has eight date-eligible records and six ingredient/route/unit rows. Independently specified arithmetic expects 25 mg × 0.5 tablet × 1/day + 5 mg/mL × 2 mL × 3/day = **42.5 mg known scheduled**, and a separately retained 10 mg × 2 tablets × 3/day = **60 mg optional PRN ceiling**. Different routes and IU remain separate; missing PRN limits and unresolved schedules remain null/incomplete. Other cases cover first/last-day boundaries, held/stopped/unknown-start exclusions, g/mcg conversion, label-versus-alias matching, duplicate component rejection, missing quantities, invalid/fractional frequencies, calendar errors, bad backups, source/report provenance and CSV formula protection. A 5,000-record reference reconciles exactly to **62,500 mg** in an invented arithmetic-only case and retains every contribution.

All 85 tests, TypeScript checking and production build pass. Browser evidence, including actual manual save/edit/remove/undo, reviewed restoration, exports and responsive inspection, is recorded in `docs/validation/medication-plan-ui-2026-11-22.json`. These are deterministic bookkeeping validations on invented data, not clinical validation or reproduction of any model's published accuracy. User visual acceptance remains pending.
