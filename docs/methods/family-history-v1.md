# Family Hub source-history method v1

Method `symphony-family-history-v1` · implemented/reviewed 2026-11-23. The current Symphony AI copper/ivory UI remains a proposal awaiting explicit user visual acceptance.

## Endpoint and ownership model

The unit is a stable **Family Hub profile ID**, with its own manually entered source assertions and parent/caregiver references. Names are display aliases, not identity keys; same names do not merge people. The optional index person is a navigation choice, not an automatically identified patient or an association to previously uploaded data. Existing glucose, genome, laboratory, medication and Medical Records stores remain **unassigned and unchanged**. Profiles isolate family histories as data containers inside this module; they do not provide encryption, authentication, user permissions or whole-app person switching.

The descriptive endpoint is the selected person's number of source records, dated records, explicit assertion categories and exact-label groups with mixed assertions. No population disease estimand, prevalence, clinical screening completeness, heritability, genetic relatedness, inheritance probability or disease-risk estimate is produced. There is no inferential uncertainty interval or clinical classifier. Missing information is represented directly rather than imputed.

The Family Hub page's previous fictional relatives and unsupported clinical/genetic recommendations have been removed. Other explicitly marked demonstration sections remain separate and are not inputs to this workflow.

## Contract and validity gates

- Strict entry/wrapper schemas: `symphony-ledger-v1` / `family-profiles`, unique profile IDs, at most 200 people, 1,000 source-history records per person and 10,000 across the workspace. These are computational limits, not a guarantee that every maximum-size dataset will fit the browser's storage quota. Storage failure leaves the earlier dataset intact and is surfaced.
- Optional birth/death **years**, explicit unknown/living/deceased status, profile source, optional source URL, notes and source-check instant. Years are constrained to 1000–9999 and cannot follow the profile's UTC source-check year. Death year requires deceased status and cannot precede birth year. No full birth date, sex, legal identity or genomic file is required/inferred.
- Entered links identify existing parent/caregiver profiles with explicit `biological`, `adoptive`, `step` or `guardian` type and their own source/URL. Self-links, duplicate links to the same person, unresolved references and directed cycles are rejected. This model supports at most two entered biological parents. With known birth years, an entered biological parent must have an earlier year; no age/sex constraint is inferred for social links.
- Reference/cycle validation uses an independently implemented Kahn topological traversal, O(V+E), over **all** entered parent/caregiver edges. Disconnected profiles, missing parentage, one recorded biological parent and common ancestry with an undirected pedigree loop are allowed if the directed graph is acyclic. Missing parents are not synthesized or treated as unrelated founders. Deleting a referenced person is blocked until the user explicitly removes dependent links; no cascade silently destroys relationships.
- Source assertions retain exact condition labels, `reported present` / `explicitly denied` / `unknown`, optional source assessment date, optional reported integer onset age in years (0–150), source/URL, notes and latest manual capture/edit instant. A known zero onset age survives; unknown stays null. Age cap is a method bound, not a clinical threshold. Onset age applies only to a reported-present record.
- Assessment dates must be valid ISO calendar dates and cannot follow the UTC capture date; this conservative calendar gate does not establish the original source's time zone. Known birth/assessment/death years gate grossly inconsistent onset ages; year precision cannot establish an exact birthday, age or chronology within a year. Retrospective documentation after death is permitted, with onset constrained by the known death-year context. Capture/edit time never substitutes for assessment time.
- Profile and history editors capture a snapshot of the saved workspace. A changed stored snapshot blocks an old draft, retaining it for review. History drafts also carry the selected person's ID; a changed owner cannot silently reassign the record. This is a best-effort pre-write check, **not an atomic cross-tab transaction or lock**. Backups and single-browser editing remain advisable for concurrent use. Selecting another person intentionally clears the unsaved history form, as stated in the UI.

## Analysis and interpretation

`reviewFamilyProfile` works on one profile's history only. It counts source records with a supplied assessment date and each explicitly entered assertion. A person with no records has unknown coverage, not a negative finding. Dated-record counts are denominated by entered source records, not a list of all diseases that ought to have been assessed.

Labels are folded for case and repeated whitespace only. A label group containing at least one reported-present and one explicitly-denied record gets a **mixed source assertions** inspection flag. Different source dates, definitions, transcription errors or changes over time can explain this heterogeneity. It is not an adjudicated contradiction, diagnosis or present-day clinical state. Unknown timing stays visible. Aliases, disease ontologies, related disease concepts, clinical negation and natural language are not automatically interpreted. Matching labels in different profiles never combine their clinical observations.

Parent/caregiver links are explicitly reported source data, not genetic confirmation. The connection inspector shows entered parents and children and preserves social link types. It is not a conventional clinical pedigree drawing, relationship inference engine, CanRisk/BOADICEA model or genetic test. No diploid inheritance, penetrance, Mendelian disease, PGx or offspring analysis uses these links.

## Interface, persistence and output scope

Add/edit person profiles and source histories, search/select people, inspect relationships, remove/undo and review JSON backups locally. People paginate 12 per page and history records 20. Profile edits preserve that person's saved history. Removing a profile removes only its own contained history and can be undone; incoming relationship references prevent deletion. History removal also has an owner-aware undo.

Storage uses unencrypted browser localStorage at `symphony:family-profiles:v1`. It is not a secure clinical vault. There is no account separation, access-control enforcement, server sync or third-party upload. Use aliases where appropriate, retain sources and review before sharing.

- **Selected-person report:** `symphony-family-person-report-v1` includes the selected profile, its owned history, source metadata, entered relationship IDs and a method/coverage summary. It never expands another person's profile or history through a link. Free text/source notes can still name other people as entered; export scope is not text de-identification. No other personal uploads/ledgers are included.
- **Selected-person CSV:** one row per owned history record, including person ID/label, assertion, original assessment date, onset age, source, source URL, capture time, notes and method. Missing date/age values remain empty. Existing CSV formula-prefix escaping is retained.
- **Whole-workspace backup:** explicitly includes all Family Hub profiles/history. Restore is reviewed before replacement and runs schema, identity and graph validation; rejected restore preserves current records. Raw backup remains available for unreadable data; exporting an apparently valid JSON backup is disabled in that state while repair/restore stays available.
- **Main personal report:** includes Family Hub **counts and scope only**. Names, relationship details and owned histories are excluded. This avoids attaching relatives' histories to the unassigned personal workspace. Unreadable/unready Family Hub storage blocks full report download instead of silently emitting misleading counts.

## References and selection decisions

Three references selected and accessed 2026-11-23; no application source code, model weights or clinical/genome data copied.

1. **HL7 FHIR R4 v4.0.1 [FamilyMemberHistory definitions](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/familymemberhistory-definitions.html)**. Reviewed person/relative attribution, absent-history information, record date, onset/age context and source semantics. Adopted explicit ownership and missingness distinctions; our schema and assertion categories are independent and **not FHIR conformance or a FHIR import/export mapping**. HL7's [specification license](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/license.html) governs reuse; only a design/method reference was used.
2. **[pedigreejs](https://github.com/CCGE-BOADICEA/pedigreejs/tree/daa9faa61634b4f57279c7478cc918ee3fe0274c)**, pinned commit `daa9faa61634b4f57279c7478cc918ee3fe0274c`; [validation in es/utils.js](https://github.com/CCGE-BOADICEA/pedigreejs/blob/daa9faa61634b4f57279c7478cc918ee3fe0274c/es/utils.js). README, GPL-3.0 license and identity/reference validation inspected. Adopted the principle of validating relationships before use. No GPL source reused, dependency installed or clinical pedigree-notation equivalence claimed. We intentionally do not enforce its complete mother/father pair and sex fields; unknown parentage and source-described social links remain explicit in our own bounded graph model.
3. **[Extraction of Family History Information From Clinical Notes: Deep Learning and Heuristics Approach](https://medinform.jmir.org/2020/12/e22898)**, JMIR Medical Informatics 2020, DOI 10.2196/22898, published article (CC BY). Reviewed the n2c2 entity/relation tasks, training/test separation, hybrid extraction methods, evaluation tables and negation limitations. Person attribution, observations and negation are separate failure modes; identifying a disease word does not establish whose condition it is or its clinical truth. This informed explicit person ownership and assertions. No corpus/model installed, test set reproduced or published F1 transferred to Symphony's manual-entry workflow.

Additional screening (not integration): [MedExtractor Hugging Face card](https://huggingface.co/lion-ai/medextractor-nano/tree/502b731aeafe44ae802f87134c7b969d0d7acb9f), unchanged revision `502b731aeafe44ae802f87134c7b969d0d7acb9f`, was rechecked. The revision calls itself MedExtractor Liquid, reports synthetic-only clinical evaluation and non-commercial/research restrictions; its extraction and relation performance were not evaluated locally and no weights/code were run. arXiv and bioRxiv searches did not identify a better matched, independently validated method to adopt for this bounded graph/source release. Preprint/model novelty was not used as evidence of clinical validity.

## Local reference validation

`tests/family-history.test.ts` adds 14 cases. A hand-specified six-profile/six-history fixture has distinct biological/guardian links, empty history, an unknown-date source and opposing historical assertions. The selected Parent A profile has 3 records, 2 dated records, 1 reported-present, 1 denial, 1 unknown and 1 mixed exact-label group; other people's matching labels do not change these counts. Zero onset age, missing dates, multiple aliases, unresolved/deleted parents, directed versus undirected cycles, index uniqueness, source chronology, invalid backups, stale drafts and ownership changes have positive/negative references.

Export tests check that another profile's sentinel name/history never appears in a selected-person output or main personal report. The 200-profile/10,000-history reference retains exact aggregate counts under bounded graph validation. All 99 tests, typechecking and production build pass. Browser workflow/desktop/mobile checks and screenshots are in `docs/validation/family-history-ui-2026-11-23.json` and `docs/screenshots/symphony-family-*`.

These establish declared bookkeeping, structural and export-scope behavior on invented data. They do not authenticate people, source assertions or biological relationships, reproduce an NLP benchmark, establish clinical accuracy or validate disease-risk prediction. User visual acceptance remains pending.
